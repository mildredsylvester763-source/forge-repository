# AI-049: Provider failover and automatic retries

## Behaviour

- Three strikes: each failed call increments the provider's consecutiveFailures counter, and at three the provider is marked unhealthy. A healthy call resets the counter to zero.
- Failover builds a retry chain from the remaining healthy providers, up to maxRetriesPerProvider attempts each, and names the unhealthy providers it excluded. Recovery is a first-class event, not an accident.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/model-gateway-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/gateway/call and /v1/ai-training/gateway/failover.

## Limits, stated plainly

- Health is a local counter per provider, not a real probe of the outside service. A provider can be counted unhealthy here while being perfectly fine in the world.
