# AI-023: Creative evals with human panels

## Behaviour

- Creative cases are scored by named human panelists, one vote each; duplicates are refused with panel-duplicate-panelist.
- Aggregation is a quorum-gated median, order-independent by construction; scores are 0..100.
- Refusals named: panel-no-votes, panel-invalid-quorum, panel-vote-invalid, panel-invalid-score, panel-quorum-unmet, panel-case-unknown.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/eval-gate-engine.ts (pure) plus service vote storage; POST /v1/ai-training/eval/panel/vote and POST /v1/ai-training/eval/panel/aggregate.

## Limits, stated plainly

The median is honest but blunt; panels smaller than quorum produce no verdict at all. The engine cannot tell whether panelists are independent people.
