# AI-068: Copyright-safe quoting limits

## Behaviour

- Five named licenses: public-domain, cc0, cc-by, owned, no-ai. The no-ai license forbids verbatim quoting outright.
- Hard caps: at most 3 quotes per request, at most 50 words per quote, at most 100 quoted words in total. Quotes are trimmed; words are non-whitespace tokens.
- Validation order is fixed and named: quote-license-missing, quote-license-unknown, quotes-empty, quote-verbatim-forbidden, quote-count-exceeded, quote-text-missing, quote-too-long, quote-total-exceeded.

## Where it lives

src/modules/search-integrity/engine/quoting-limits.ts (pure), reached through GET /v1/search-integrity/quotes/licenses and POST /v1/search-integrity/quotes.

## Limits, stated plainly

Word counting is whitespace splitting, and paraphrase is never checked. The license declared is the license trusted; the engine does not verify what the source actually says.
