# AI-066: Research mode reports

## Behaviour

- Research plans use the canonical step order search, read, cross-check, draft. Subsets are allowed in order; the route lowercases and trims step names.
- Plan refusals are named: research-topic-missing, research-steps-not-array, research-steps-empty, research-step-unknown, research-step-duplicate, research-steps-out-of-order.
- Reports carry findings of a claim plus its sources. A finding with two or more distinct sources is verified. Finding sources are kept as given, never deduplicated; questions are trimmed and blank ones refused.
- Reports persist per user with a generated summary and can be listed and fetched by id: research-report-not-found when missing.

## Where it lives

src/modules/workspace-retrieval/engine/research-mode.ts (pure), reached through GET /v1/workspace-retrieval/research/steps, POST /v1/workspace-retrieval/research/plan, and the research/reports routes.

## Limits, stated plainly

The planner is a checklist, not an autonomous researcher, and verification counts sources rather than checking truth. Two copied sentences from one site still verify a finding.
