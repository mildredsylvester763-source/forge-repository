# TQ-003: Android device testing

## Behaviour

- The android platform supports exactly ten actions: tap, swipe, long-press, type, scroll, back, home, rotate, install, assert. Gestures pinch and multi-touch exist on touch platforms and belong to the gesture catalog.
- Unknown platforms are refused with platform-unknown:x; an empty action with platform-action-missing; an action no platform knows with platform-action-unknown:x; an action another platform knows with platform-action-unsupported:action:android.

## Where it lives

src/modules/live-testing/engine/platform-capability.ts (pure), enforced on every recorded step through POST /v1/live-testing/sessions/steps.

## Limits, stated plainly

The engine validates the android vocabulary; it does not own a device farm. Physical devices are a separate, flagged concern.
