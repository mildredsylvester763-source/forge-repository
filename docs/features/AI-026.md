# AI-026: Claim grounding

## Behaviour

- Claims are grounded against facts by normalized containment in either direction; every claim id ends up grounded or ungrounded, and sources used are named and sorted.
- The ungrounded count is capped by maxUngrounded; exceeding the cap refuses the whole report (grounding-limit-exceeded), never trims it silently.
- Refusals named: grounding-no-claims, grounding-invalid-limit, grounding-claim-no-id, grounding-claim-no-text, grounding-duplicate-claim, grounding-fact-no-id, grounding-fact-no-text, grounding-duplicate-fact.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/safety-eval-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/safety/ground.

## Limits, stated plainly

Containment is not reasoning; a claim restating a fact in different words stays ungrounded. The engine has no world model and no retrieval.
