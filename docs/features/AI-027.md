# AI-027: Training data contamination detection

## Behaviour

- Contamination is detected by n-gram containment: the fraction of a text n-grams found in a corpus document; flagged documents always meet the threshold, ratios rounded to a millionth.
- Defaults are n 5 and threshold 0.9; n is bounded 3..12.
- Refusals named: contamination-no-text, contamination-no-corpus, contamination-invalid-n, contamination-invalid-threshold, contamination-doc-no-id, contamination-doc-no-text, contamination-duplicate-doc, contamination-text-too-short.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/safety-eval-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/safety/contamination.

## Limits, stated plainly

Word overlap, not semantic overlap; a paraphrased leak scores low. Very short texts are refused rather than judged, so they cannot be checked at all.
