# AI-042: Latency, throughput, and cost monitoring per model

## Behaviour

- Percentiles are computed over sorted latencies of successful requests only - failed requests have no honest latency - and p50 <= p95 <= p99 always. Error rate is the honest share of failed requests.
- Cost per 1k tokens is derived from totals, never invented. A p95 over the latency budget sets budgetBreached: degradation is flagged, not hidden. Invalid samples are refused with their index (metrics-invalid-sample).

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/model-operations-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/ops/metrics.

## Limits, stated plainly

- Samples are pushed by the caller in batches. No automatic collection from a real serving stack exists yet.
