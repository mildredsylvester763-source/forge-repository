# TQ-024: Session collaboration

## Behaviour

- Three roles with a fixed capability matrix: owner (lifecycle, steps, messages, findings, control), tester (steps, messages, findings) and observer (messages only).
- Every capability check is answered by the matrix with named refusals: collaboration-role-unknown:X, collaboration-capability-unknown:X, collaboration-capability-denied:R:C.
- Invitations walk a small state machine: invited accepts into active or revokes into revoked; any other move is refused by name (collaboration-state-invalid, collaboration-state-unknown, collaboration-action-unknown).
- Owners act on their sessions; active participants act within their role; strangers get session-not-found.

## Where it lives

src/modules/live-testing/engine/session-collaboration.ts (pure), wired through the participant routes (invite, accept, revoke, list), GET /v1/live-testing/collaboration/roles and the capability checks on step, message, finding, control and data routes.

## Limits, stated plainly

Collaboration is single workspace simple: roles are fixed, there are no custom roles and no per-session overrides.
