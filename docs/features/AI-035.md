# AI-035: Staged promotion: experimental, internal, beta, stable

## Behaviour

- The ladder is experimental -> internal -> beta -> stable, one step at a time. Skipping is refused by name (promotion-illegal), so nothing jumps to stable untested.
- Gates are hard and boundaries pass: internal needs an eval score, beta needs a card and eval >= 0.8, stable needs a card and safety pass rate >= 0.95. Missing evidence is refused (promotion-missing-eval, promotion-missing-card, promotion-missing-safety); failed gates are refused loudly with both numbers (promotion-gate-failed).
- Every promotion is recorded with its own fingerprint (promofp_ prefix), so the promotion history is auditable.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/model-registry-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/models/promote.

## Limits, stated plainly

- Gates are thresholds, not judgment. A model can pass every gate and still be wrong; the gates only keep untested things out of stable.
