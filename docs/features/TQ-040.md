# TQ-040: Cross-browser and cross-device visual matrix

## Behaviour

- A matrix is the cartesian product of browsers (chrome, firefox, safari, edge), platforms (desktop, android, ios) and viewports (1280x720, 1920x1080, 375x667, 768x1024); the full default plan is 44 combos.
- Impossible market combinations (Safari on Android) are excluded from the plan and counted as unsupportedSkipped rather than planned. Coverage is the share of combos actually tested (pass + fail); skips are not tested.
- A matrix meets its threshold only when coverage reaches it AND no combo failed. Malformed input is refused by name: matrix-browsers-missing, matrix-platforms-missing, matrix-viewports-missing, matrix-browser-unknown:x, matrix-platform-unknown:x, matrix-viewport-unknown:x, matrix-combos-empty, matrix-combo-limit:n, matrix-combo-id-missing:i, matrix-results-invalid, matrix-combo-unknown:x, matrix-result-missing:x, matrix-status-invalid:x:id, matrix-threshold-invalid:x.

## Where it lives

src/modules/test-quality/engine/browser-matrix.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/test-quality/matrix/plan and POST /v1/test-quality/matrix/score. GET /v1/test-quality/matrix/catalogs lists the catalogs.

## Limits, stated plainly

The engine plans and scores the matrix; it does not drive real browsers. Execution against a device cloud is a later feature.
