# AI-028: Blind side-by-side comparison

## Behaviour

- Votes are cast blind as a, b or tie, one per named voter; duplicate voters are a 409 conflict named blind-duplicate-voter.
- The tally counts preferences and either meets quorum or not; below quorum the winner is undecided, never a guess.
- Refusals named: blind-no-votes, blind-invalid-quorum, blind-voter-no-id, blind-invalid-preference.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/safety-eval-engine.ts (pure) plus service ballot storage; POST /v1/ai-training/blind/vote and POST /v1/ai-training/blind/tally.

## Limits, stated plainly

Blind means the ballot stores no reason; a tied contest stays tied. The engine cannot verify voters actually saw the artifacts unlabelled.
