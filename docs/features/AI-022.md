# AI-022: FORGE benchmark grading

## Behaviour

- Forge cases are graded against explicit per-case criteria; the score is a weighted mean of per-case criteria pass rates, weights defaulting to 1.
- Iteration is key-sorted, so the verdict is deterministic under any payload serialization order.
- Refusals named: grade-case-unknown, grade-case-missing, grade-result-invalid, grade-criterion-unknown, grade-criterion-duplicate, grade-creative-not-criteria.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/eval-gate-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/eval/grade.

## Limits, stated plainly

Pass or fail per criterion is declared by the caller; the engine grades nothing itself. Creative suites are refused here by design, they belong to the panel flow.
