# TQ-028: Audio and video output checks

## Behaviour

- Codec support is tabulated per platform: web plays h264, vp9 and av1 video with aac, opus and mp3 audio; android and ios play h264 and hevc with smaller audio sets; desktop plays everything including flac.
- Playback checks refuse by name: av-platform-missing, av-platform-unknown:X, av-kind-missing, av-kind-unknown:X, av-codec-missing, av-codec-unknown:X, av-codec-wrong-kind:X, av-codec-unsupported:C:P.
- Sync drift is banded on the ITU-R BT.1359-1 thresholds: in-sync, drifting past +45 or -125 ms, out-of-sync past +90 or -185 ms, with detectable and acceptable flags.
- Media state moves are a fixed machine: idle to playing, playing to paused or ended, paused back to playing; from is validated before to, and refusals are named (av-state-missing, av-state-unknown:X, av-state-refused:F:T).

## Where it lives

src/modules/live-testing/engine/av-output.ts (pure), POST /v1/live-testing/av/codecs for the catalog and POST /v1/live-testing/sessions/av-playback, /av-sync and /av-state for the live checks.

## Limits, stated plainly

Checks are catalog and threshold logic; no media is decoded and no clock is measured.
