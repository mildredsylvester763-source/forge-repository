# AI-012: Tokenizer training

## Behaviour

- The tokenizer trains deterministically from a corpus: the initial vocabulary is the corpus alphabet, sorted; training merges the most frequent adjacent pair, ties break lexicographically; merges are recorded in order.
- The content hash is derived from the corpus, never supplied, so the same corpus plus vocabSize always yields the same tokenizer. vocabSize must sit between the alphabet size and 1024; training stops early when no pairs remain.
- Encoding applies merges in training order and maps tokens to vocabulary indices; decoding joins them back. Encode then decode round-trips the corpus exactly.
- Registration is idempotent per (corpus, vocabSize): a duplicate manifest returns the existing tokenizer with duplicate true.

## Where it lives

src/modules/training-data/engine/tokenizer-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/training-data/tokenizer/register, /encode, /decode and /list.

## Limits, stated plainly

Encoding refuses any character outside the corpus alphabet by name; there is no fallback token. Token ids are private to the owner's account. Training is deliberately simple and full-corpus, so its cost grows with corpus length and the requested vocab size.
