# TQ-026: Gesture mechanics

## Behaviour

- Seven gestures with physics: tap, swipe, long-press, pinch, rotate, multi-touch and stylus-pressure. ios supports all seven, android six (no stylus-pressure), web and desktop none.
- Physics laws: swipe needs a direction (up, down, left or right); stylus-pressure needs 0 to 1 pressure; long-press takes 100 to 10000 ms and defaults to 500; pinch and rotate use 2 contacts; multi-touch uses 2 to 5.
- Unexpected parameters are refused, not ignored: gesture-direction-unexpected, gesture-pressure-unexpected, gesture-duration-unexpected, gesture-contacts-invalid:N and friends, all by name.
- Each verdict carries a plain text narration such as "Swiping up with 1 finger".

## Where it lives

src/modules/live-testing/engine/gesture-mechanics.ts (pure), POST /v1/live-testing/gestures for the catalog and POST /v1/live-testing/sessions/gesture for the live step (requires the steps capability and an acting session).

## Limits, stated plainly

Gesture physics are validated and narrated, not performed; no touch events reach a real device.
