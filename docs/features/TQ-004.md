# TQ-004: iOS device testing

## Behaviour

- The ios platform supports exactly eight actions: tap, swipe, long-press, type, scroll, rotate, terminate, assert. Gestures pinch, multi-touch and stylus-pressure belong to the touch gesture catalog.
- Unknown platforms are refused with platform-unknown:x; an empty action with platform-action-missing; an action no platform knows with platform-action-unknown:x; an action another platform knows with platform-action-unsupported:action:ios.

## Where it lives

src/modules/live-testing/engine/platform-capability.ts (pure), enforced on every recorded step through POST /v1/live-testing/sessions/steps.

## Limits, stated plainly

The engine validates the iOS vocabulary; it does not reserve simulators or hardware. Device provisioning is separate.
