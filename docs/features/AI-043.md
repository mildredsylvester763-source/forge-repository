# AI-043: Shadow deployment: new model runs silently beside the live one

## Behaviour

- The challenger runs beside the champion and the agreement rate over at least 30 pairs decides: at or above the threshold (default 0.95) the verdict is promote-shadow, below it the shadow holds.
- Under-floor samples are refused by name (shadow-insufficient-samples): a verdict from thin air is a guess, and this engine does not guess. Champion and challenger cannot be the same model (shadow-same-model).

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/model-operations-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/ops/shadow.

## Limits, stated plainly

- Agreement measures matching outputs, not matching quality. Both models can be wrong together and agree.
