# AI-072: Offline knowledge packs

## Behaviour

- Packs are named, topic-scoped collections built within a size budget in kilobytes, default 10240, range 1..200000.
- Articles are validated (title, topic in pack, size between 1 and the budget), then sorted by pack topic order, size descending, title ascending, and selected greedily until the budget is full.
- A deterministic FNV-1a checksum is derived over the selected titles joined with a pipe, so a pack can be verified byte for byte later.
- Refusals are named: pack-name-missing, pack-name-too-long, pack-topics-missing, pack-topic-blank, pack-budget-invalid, pack-articles-empty, pack-article-title-missing, pack-article-topic-missing, pack-article-topic-out-of-pack, pack-article-size-invalid, pack-articles-count-invalid.

## Where it lives

src/modules/search-integrity/engine/offline-packs.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/search-integrity/packs, POST /v1/search-integrity/packs/list and POST /v1/search-integrity/packs/get.

## Limits, stated plainly

Selection is greedy, not an optimal knapsack, and article sizes are declared by the caller rather than measured. The checksum covers titles only, so body drift inside a pack is not detected.
