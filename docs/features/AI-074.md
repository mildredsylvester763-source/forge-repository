# AI-074: Task planner and plan editing

## Behaviour

- A goal becomes a linear step chain with stable ids s1..sn; every step depends on its predecessor.
- Steps carry actions from a fixed catalog (search, write, run, verify, present) with optional detail.
- Before approval the user can edit the plan with insert, remove and move operations; the chain is re-sequenced to s1..sn afterwards.
- Malformed input is refused by name: plan-goal-missing, plan-goal-too-long, plan-steps-empty, plan-steps-limit:n, plan-step-action-missing, plan-step-action-unknown:x, plan-edit-step-id-missing, plan-edit-step-duplicate:x, plan-edit-op-missing, plan-edit-op-unknown:x, plan-edit-index-invalid:x, plan-edit-id-unknown:x, plan-edit-step-missing, plan-edit-result-empty.

## Where it lives

src/modules/orchestrator/engine/task-planner.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/orchestrator/plans and POST /v1/orchestrator/plans/edit.

## Limits, stated plainly

Plans are linear by design; branching plans are out of scope for Part 01. Limits: 20 steps per plan, 500 characters per goal.
