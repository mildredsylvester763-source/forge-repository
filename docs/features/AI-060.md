# AI-060: Freshness awareness

## Behaviour

- A source is fresh within 30 days, current within a year, stale after that, and unknown when no date is present.
- Future dates are refused by name (freshness-future-date): a page that claims to be published in the future is a data error, never a freshness signal. Malformed dates are refused too (freshness-invalid-date).
- Ranking treats an invalid or missing date as the unknown band, never a faked one.

## Where it lives

src/modules/web-search/engine/freshness.ts (pure). It has no route of its own; source ranking (AI-057) and page storage (AI-058) consume it.

## Limits, stated plainly

Freshness trusts the publishedAt value; it cannot detect a page that lies about its date. Bands are coarse (30 and 365 day lines), not a continuous decay.
