# AI-087 - Uncertainty report: what the AI is unsure about

Catalog row (Part 01 With Plans): "Uncertainty report: what the AI is unsure about" - Plan from: Free, Engine: General, no pass needed. The AI part draws free credits (40 per 5 hours); the manual twin is always free.

## What it is
An honest report of every area where the AI is unsure. Each item names an area, a detail and a level (low, medium, high). An empty list is a CONFIDENT report, not an error - the engine never manufactures doubt to look humble, and never hides doubt to look strong.

## Engine
`src/modules/ai-training/engine/uncertainty-report-engine.ts` - pure.
- `buildUncertaintyReport({ subjectId, summary, items })` - a non-empty subject id and summary; items may be empty. Each item is an area, a detail and a level. `overall` is the strongest level present ('none' when confident); verdict is `confident` or `report`. The report carries a `ur_` fingerprint and never mutates the caller input.

Refusals: `uncertainty-no-subject-id`, `uncertainty-no-summary`, `uncertainty-items-not-a-list`, `uncertainty-item-invalid:<i>`, `uncertainty-no-area:<i>`, `uncertainty-no-detail:<i>`, `uncertainty-level-invalid:<level>`.

## Routes (POST, session required, gate: free)
- `/v1/ai-training/uncertainty/report` - 200, the report (verdict, overall, fingerprint) and its createdAt

## Honest state
Reports live in an in-memory session Map (the streaming-sessions precedent): a restart clears them, nothing is persisted yet. The engine judges only what the caller submits - it never invents confidence.
