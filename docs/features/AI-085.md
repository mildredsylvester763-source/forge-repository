# AI-085 - Per-task budgets: time, cost, tool calls

Catalog row (Part 01 With Plans): "Per-task budgets: time, cost, tool calls" - Plan from: Free, Engine: General, no pass needed.

## What it is
A budget carries one or more whole-number limits (timeMs, costUsdMicros, toolCalls). Charges land atomically; the crossing charge is recorded as the one that exceeded, with the field named, and every later charge is refused by name. Money stays whole smallest-unit integers.

## Engine
`src/modules/ai-training/engine/task-budget-engine.ts` - pure.
- `defineTaskBudget({ taskId, limits })` - a non-empty task id and at least one positive integer limit. Refuses empty or invalid limits by field name.
- `chargeTaskBudget(budget, charge)` - non-negative integer charges only; the charge that crosses a limit is recorded honestly (`exceeded: <field>`), and any charge after a breach answers `budget-already-exceeded:<field>`. The caller record is never mutated.

Refusals: `budget-no-task`, `budget-no-limits`, `budget-limit-invalid:<field>`, `budget-charge-invalid:<field>`, `budget-already-exceeded:<field>`.

## Routes (all POST, session required, gate: free)
- `/v1/ai-training/tasks/budget/define` - 201, the budget and its createdAt (409 task-budget-duplicate)
- `/v1/ai-training/tasks/budget/charge` - 200, the charged budget (404 task-budget-not-found)
- `/v1/ai-training/tasks/budget/status` - 200, the budget and its createdAt

## Honest state
Budgets live in an in-memory session Map (the streaming-sessions precedent): a restart clears them, nothing is persisted yet.
