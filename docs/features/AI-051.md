# AI-051: Per-request policy: which data may go to which provider

## Behaviour

- The caller names data tags and the provider's clearance; the policy answers what may be sent. A tag above the provider's clearance is refused naming both (policy-clearance-exceeded).
- The answer is explicit: allowed tags, the highest tag, and the clearance they were judged against.

## Where it lives

src/modules/ai-training/engine/model-gateway-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/ai-training/gateway/policy.

## Limits, stated plainly

- Tags are declared by the caller. The engine cannot inspect a request body for untagged secrets; that is the redaction layer's job (AI-053).
