# AI-001: Training data lake

## Behaviour

- The lake is append-only. Records are never edited or removed, and a refused admit leaves nothing behind.
- The legal floor is binary and permanent: stolen-data, pirated-dataset, illegal-content and privacy-violation are refused outright, and no score can rehabilitate them.
- Rights are source terms x consent x license. Below 0.4 the record is refused; from 0.4 up to 0.7 it is admitted to review; at 0.7 or above it is accepted. Quality never gates admission, it only weights intake: intakeWeight = qualityScore x provenance multiplier.
- Provenance confidence below 0.6 reads as unknown. Multipliers: human 1.0, ai 0.3, mixed 0.6, unknown 0.2.
- Duplicate identity is the fingerprint over sourceKind:contentHash:creator:license:parents. A duplicate admit is idempotent: the existing record comes back with duplicate true and nothing is appended.
- Lineage is a pair: derivedFrom and method, both or neither. Parents must already exist, so cycles are impossible; a record naming its own future id is refused as lake-self-parent.
- Composition enforces, in order: the ai cap (more than 15 percent refused), the diversity cap (more than 2 percent from one creator refused unless relaxed), and the human-share floor (below 85 percent the batch pauses with a flag, it is not a refusal).

## Where it lives

src/modules/training-data/engine/data-lake-engine.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/training-data/lake/admit, POST /v1/training-data/lake/compose, POST /v1/training-data/lake/lineage and POST /v1/training-data/lake/list.

## Limits, stated plainly

The lake judges declared rights scores, not actual licenses. The 2 percent diversity cap means any batch smaller than fifty records needs its creators relaxed by name. A paused batch waits for a human decision; nothing composes it automatically.
