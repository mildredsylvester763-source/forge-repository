# TQ-015: Session recording timeline

## Behaviour

- Recorded steps and lifecycle markers merge into one deterministic timeline: every entry carries an offset from the session start, entries sort by offset, and at the same offset a step always comes before a marker. Event ids are event-1..event-n; the duration is the last offset, or zero when nothing happened.
- Step sequence numbers must be contiguous from 1; nothing may be recorded before the session started.
- Refusals are named: recording-not-started, recording-step-seq-broken:n, recording-step-bad-time:n, recording-step-before-start:n, recording-marker-label-empty, recording-marker-bad-time, recording-marker-before-start.

## Where it lives

src/modules/live-testing/engine/session-recording.ts (pure), reached through POST /v1/live-testing/sessions/recording.

## Limits, stated plainly

The timeline is built from what was recorded, in session order. It is a review artifact, not a video; it cannot prove things that were never recorded.
